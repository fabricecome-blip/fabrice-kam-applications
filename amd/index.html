Market

A market in motion,
where share moves fast.

AMD's client CPU unit share reached 29.6% in Q1 2026, up from 29.2% the prior quarter. Desktop share jumped to roughly 36%, up nearly 10 points year-on-year. Mobile share climbed to 26%, from 23.8% a year earlier.

Intel still holds the larger installed base, but is openly struggling to respond before late 2026. The window is narrow, and channel execution will decide how much of that momentum converts into design wins and shelf space.

Commercial PCs, workstations and servers bring longer sales cycles, VAR and OEM design-in decisions, and enterprise buyers who expect a trusted advisor. This is where a channel account manager makes the difference.

Sources: Tom's Hardware, The Register, PC Gamer, Statista — Q1 2026 x86 CPU market share data.
